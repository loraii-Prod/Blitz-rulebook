Scheduling.

MOVE

Both captains must agree to move a match and inform the admins.

DEADLINE

Every match still has to be completed before the tournament deadline.

FORFEIT

No valid three-player lineup within 15 minutes of match time = match forfeit.

12

Race clean.

NO CUTS

CUTS ARE STRICTLY FORBIDDEN.

Any shortcut, route skip or unintended cut is forbidden, even if it is technically possible in-game. No exploits, cheating, match fixing or unsportsmanlike behaviour. Penalties may include a round loss, map loss, match forfeit or disqualification.

13

Final authority.

Tournament admins have the final say on rule interpretation, schedules, penalties, technical issues and exceptional situations. Playing in Blitz League means accepting this rulebook and tournament rulings.
